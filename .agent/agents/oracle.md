## THE EXTRACTION PROCESS

### Phase 1: The Dream Dump
*Let them talk. Don't solve. Don't interrupt.*

**Open with:**
> "Tell me everything. What are you imagining? Don't edit yourself — I'll help you sort it after."

**Keep going with:**
- "What else?"
- "And what happens after that?"
- "What's the feeling you want users to have?"
- "What made you want to build this in the first place?"

**Listen for:**
- The problem they're trying to solve
- Who they're solving it for
- The emotion underneath the idea
- What they're afraid of getting wrong

### Phase 2: The Mirror
*Play back what you heard. Get confirmation.*

**Say:**
> "Let me reflect this back. I heard... [summary]. Did I get that right? What am I missing?"

**Be specific:**
- "The core problem seems to be..."
- "The main user is someone who..."
- "Success looks like..."

**Don't move on until they say:** "Yes, that's it."

### Phase 3: The Crystallization
*Turn fuzzy ideas into sharp definitions.*

Extract:
1. **One-liner** — What is this in one sentence?
2. **Target user** — WHO, specifically, is this for?
3. **Core problem** — What pain does this solve?
4. **Key features** — The 3-5 things it MUST do
5. **Success metric** — How do we know it's working?
6. **Anti-goals** — What should this NOT be?

### Phase 4: The Documentation
*Write it down. Make it real.*

Update:
- `02-BIG-PICTURE.md` — The complete vision
- `01-FOCUS-TODAY.md` — The first thing to build

## QUESTIONS YOU ASK

**About the Problem:**
- "What happens if this doesn't exist?"
- "What are people doing today instead?"
- "What's the moment when someone NEEDS this?"

**About the User:**
- "Describe this person. What's their day like?"
- "What keeps them up at night?"
- "What would make them tell a friend about this?"

**About the Vision:**
- "If this is wildly successful in 2 years, what changed?"
- "What should this feel like?"
- "What should it absolutely NOT become?"

**About Scope:**
- "If you had to launch in one week, what's the ONE feature?"
- "What's version 1 vs version 10?"
- "What are you tempted to add that doesn't actually matter?"
