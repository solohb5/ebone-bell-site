## PARKED IDEAS

### 1. The Batman Toggle
**What:** Site toggles between "Business Mode" and "Body Mode" with different color schemes
**Why parked:** Overcomplicating. The fork handles this better. Let users choose, don't make the site transform.
**Status:** Killed

---

### 2. Animated Text Sequences
**What:** Hero text typing out or revealing dramatically
**Why parked:** Edge is in the copy, not the gimmicks. Animations feel try-hard.
**Status:** Killed

---

### 3. Techy Labels / Grid Overlays
**What:** "SYS.", "DIAGNOSTIC", architectural overlays, etc.
**Why parked:** Was from an earlier direction. Doesn't match the warm, human Tommy brand.
**Status:** Killed

---

### 4. POST-FITNESS Brand Name
**What:** Positioning Tommy as "POST-FITNESS" — the last stop
**Why parked:** Too conceptual. "Tommy Reynolds" is the brand. The positioning ("protocol over motivation") is embedded in the copy.
**Status:** Evolved into current positioning

---

### 5. Detailed Offer Breakdowns
**What:** Showing package details, pricing tiers, what's included
**Why parked:** Everything goes to Calendly. Details handled on the call. Site's job is to build trust and get the booking.
**Status:** Parked — may revisit if Tommy wants self-service offers later

---

### 6. Fork Section Headlines — Evolution
**What:** Various headlines for the fork section
**Tried:**
- "What's falling apart?" — Too negative, dwells on the problem
- "Which one's falling apart?" — Same issue
- "What do you want to fix?" — Too soft, customer service vibe
- "Pick your protocol." — Good but not conversational
**Landed on:** "Where do we start?" — Collaborative, forward-looking, invites them in
**Status:** Resolved

---

### 7. Industrial/Cold Visual Direction
**What:** Concrete, graphite, harsh contrast, industrial vibe
**Why parked:** Evolved to warmer palette (Bone, Warm Stone, Sand) that feels more like a mentor's office than a construction site
**Status:** Evolved

---

### 8. The "Lost 3 Gyms" Narrative
**What:** Original story said Tommy lost 3 gyms in 2008 because he was "a great trainer but terrible businessman"
**Why parked:** NOT TRUE. Research showed: Tommy was in real estate in 2008. He lost his career in the housing crash, THEN found fitness. He never lost gyms — he built them.
**Status:** Fixed. Story now accurately reflects: real estate crash → found fitness → built 8 gyms
**Important:** This was a fabricated narrative that sounded good but wasn't Tommy's actual story. Always verify against intake + research.

---

### 9. Telegram-Style Copy
**What:** Ultra-short 2-3 word sentences everywhere
**Why parked:** Overcorrected when trying to be "punchy." Lost the storytelling soul. Site needs full sentences that flow like someone actually talking.
**Status:** Fixed. Brought back narrative voice while keeping facts accurate.
