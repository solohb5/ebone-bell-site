## THE STORY (Accurate Version)

Tommy Reynolds:
- 2008: Had career in real estate, housing market crashed, lost everything
- Found fitness at rock bottom — not a backup, his calling
- Started as a one-on-one trainer, learned the craft
- Built 8 gyms with wife Vanessa in San Diego
- $20M+ grossed over his career
- Now coaches two audiences

He helps two types of people:
1. **Trainers** who are great at fitness but struggling with business
2. **Executives** who are great at business but letting their bodies fall apart

**Core insight:** Both audiences rely on willpower, which eventually runs out. Tommy replaces willpower with systems.
